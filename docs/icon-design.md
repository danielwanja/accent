# App icon

The production asset is `Accent/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`:
1024 × 1024, opaque RGB. The OS supplies the rounded mask. The shared universal
asset is used for all appearances.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, followed by a standard resize
for the Xcode asset catalog. No third-party logo or reference artwork was used.

Design prompt: a minimal sculptural lowercase á for Accent, with a subtle
speech-opening counter; warm ivory porcelain on rich terracotta red; thick soft
strokes, restrained bevel and studio light, readable at small sizes, no words,
microphone, flags, border, watermark, or pre-rounded outer tile.

Final edit prompt: preserve the ivory glyph and shading, make the entire square
fully opaque terracotta (#B64034), center the glyph at approximately 70% of the
image height, add generous margin, and use a nearly flat warm background.
